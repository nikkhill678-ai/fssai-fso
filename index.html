Five Critical Section Distinction Traps

Section 31 vs Section 32

Section 31: Licensing & Registration scheme for Food Business Operators.
Section 32: Improvement Notice issued by the Designated Officer (minimum 14 days compliance period).

Section 33 vs Section 34

Section 33: Prohibition Order (court-linked following conviction).
Section 34: Emergency Prohibition Order (immediate health risk without waiting for final conviction).

Section 36 vs 37 vs 38

Section 36: Designated Officer (District-level officer, rank of SDO or above).
Section 37: Appointment of Food Safety Officer (appointed by CFS by notification).
Section 38: Field powers of the Food Safety Officer (inspection, sampling, seizure).

Section 42 vs Section 47

Section 42: Procedure for launching prosecution.
Section 47: Sampling and analysis methodology (divided into 4 parts).

Section 68 vs Section 70

Section 68: Adjudicating Officer (Additional District Magistrate rank).
Section 70: Food Safety Appellate Tribunal (presided by District Judge or former District Judge).
